DEFINITIONS
Solactive Genomics Index The Solactive Genomics Index tracks the companies that generate revenues from Genomics related business. Genomics business
operations include the following: (i) gene editing, (ii) genomic sequencing, (iii) development and testing of genetic medicine/therapies, (iv)
computational genomics and genetic diagnostics, and/or (v) biotechnology.
(1) International Society for Cell and Gene Therapy, Feb 2019
