REASONS TO CONSIDER
High Growth Potential
Spurred by breakthroughs in generative AI, the global artificial intelligence market is projected to
reach $184B in 2024, with the market size expected to grow to $826.7B by 2030.(1)
Rapid Commercialization
AI is enabling innovative applications in diverse sectors, including Agriculture, Health Care, and
beyond. Forecasts indicate that over 729M individuals will use AI tools by 2030, up 254M from 2023.
(2)
Unconstrained Approach
AI spans multiple segments, and its most innovative companies include both household names and
newcomers from around the world. AIQ invests accordingly, without regard for sector or geography.
