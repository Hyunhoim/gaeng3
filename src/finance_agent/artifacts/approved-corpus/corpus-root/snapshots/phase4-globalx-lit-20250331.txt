DEFINITIONS
Solactive Global Lithium Index The Solactive Global Lithium Index tracks the performance of the largest and most liquid listed companies active in exploration and/or
mining of Lithium or the production of Lithium batteries.
Lithium Lithium is a chemical element which has several industrial applications including serving as the primary component to manufacture
batteries. While there are no financial instruments tracking the direct price of lithium, the fund gains exposure to the asset class through
companies active in the exploration, mining and refinement of lithium.
