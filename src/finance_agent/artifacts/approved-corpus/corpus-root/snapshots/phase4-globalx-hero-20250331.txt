DEFINITIONS
Solactive Video Games & Esports Index The Solactive Video Games & Esports Index is designed to provide exposure to exchange-listed companies that are positioned to benefit
from increased consumption related to video games and esports, including companies whose principal business is in video game
development/publishing, video game and esports content distribution and streaming, operating/owning esports leagues/teams, and
producing video game/esports hardware. To be eligible for the Solactive Video Games & Esports Index, a company is considered by the
Index Provider to be a Video Games & Esports Company if the company generates at least 50% of its revenues from video games and
esports activities, as determined by the Index Provider.
