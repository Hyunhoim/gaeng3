DEFINITIONS
Solactive Autonomous & Electric
Vehicles Index
The Solactive Autonomous & Electric Vehicles Index tracks the price movements in shares of companies which are active in the electric
vehicles and autonomous driving segments. This particularly includes electric vehicle manufacturers, electric vehicle component producers,
companies that mine or produce raw materials that are relevant to the electric vehicle and autonomous vehicle technology segment,
companies that build autonomous vehicles, and suppliers of autonomous vehicle technologies.
