DEFINITIONS
Solactive Global Uranium & Nuclear
Components Total Return Index
The Solactive Global Uranium & Nuclear Components Total Return Index tracks the price movements in shares of companies which are (or
are expected to be in the near future) active in the uranium industry. This particularly includes uranium mining, exploration, uranium
investments and technologies related to the uranium industry. The Index will include a minimum of 20 components at every rebalancing.
