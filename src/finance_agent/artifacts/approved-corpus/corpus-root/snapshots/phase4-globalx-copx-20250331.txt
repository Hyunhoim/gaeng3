Solactive Global Copper Miners Total
Return Index
The Solactive Global Copper Miners Total Return Index is designed to reflect the performance of the copper mining industry. It is comprised
of selected companies globally that are actively engaged in some aspect of the copper mining industry, such as copper mining, refining, or
exploration.
