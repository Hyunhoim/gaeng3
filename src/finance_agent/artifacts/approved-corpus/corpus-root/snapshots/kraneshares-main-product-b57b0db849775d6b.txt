KraneShares California Carbon Allowance Strategy ETF
KCCA is a carbon ETF providing targeted exposure to the California Carbon Allowance
(CCA) cap-and-invest program, one of the world's most liquid and fastest-growing carbon
markets. KCCA is benchmarked to the S&P Carbon Credit CCA Excess Return Index, which
tracks the most traded CCA futures contracts.
Why KCCA?
Structural growth opportunity: Access an asset class with regulatory-driven supply
constraints that help support long-term structural upside potential.
Diversification: Low correlations to major assets, including just 0.1 to US large cap.1
Inflation resilience: Potential inflation hedge with a downside floor that rises annually
by 5% plus the Consumer Price Index (CPI).
Market impact: Supports price discovery and market liquidity in one of the state’s
leading tools for state-economy-wide decarbonization.
Global relevance: The California carbon allowance market covers ~80% of the state’s
greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions  across the world’s fourth-largest economy , with
nearly $60 billion in trading volume in 2025,  making it the second-largest carbon
market by trading volume globally.

What is California’s cap-and-invest market?
Established in 2013 and recently extended through 2045,  the programme operates under a declining emissions cap, tightening supply over time while demand is driven by mandatory
participation from large emitters that acquire allowances through auctions or secondary markets.
4
As government-mandated and regulated programmes, cap-and-invest creates transparent and liquid markets that incentivise companies to pursue the most cost-effective emissions
reduction strategies while driving broader industry innovation.
The programme’s annual declining cap aligns with the state’s statutory target of reducing emissions by 40% of 1990 levels by 2030 and achieving carbon neutrality by 2045.4
CCA auction proceeds fund community programs and provide utility bill rebates for households and small businesses.
